Bonk & Bait

Dad rented a boat for the family holiday. Something huge smashed it onto Splinter Cay. Fish, fight, fix the boat, and chase the beast across five islands before the late fee gets any worse.
No fish were consulted in the making of this game.
  1. Walk to the water. Hold left-click to charge a cast, let go to throw. Longer casts find rarer catches.
  2. When the bobber dunks and a big ! appears, click to hook it.
  3. Hold click to haul it in. It hangs from your rod. Walk it up the beach, away from the water.
  4. Click to drop it, then click to bonk it with your flip-flop. Crabs run for the sea, and some pinch back.
  5. Press E to pick it up before a seagull does. Sell at Captain Barb's crate, or feed a rare catch to the Skin-O-Matic for a skin.
  6. Press F at Barb's crate to shop: bait, rods, weapons, and the 3 parts that repair your boat.
  7. Follow the quest under your money. Each island ends with a boss you fish out of a glowing whirlpool.
This game needs a keyboard and mouse, so please play it on a computer.
